Sample work

Sample made by us for a made up business. Not a client project.

Made‑up audit for a boutique clothing website

The audit found 12 issues across nine pages. Three critical problems were identified: missing contrast on the product images, keyboard‑only navigation blocking the checkout button, and a form that does not announce field errors. The fix list prioritises these and includes screenshots, code snippets and a timeline for each resolution. After applying the fixes, the site meets WCAG 2.1 AA, reducing barriers for shoppers with visual and motor impairments and boosting traffic from assistive‑technology users.
