FAQ

Who is this for?

Builders, landscapers, fencing and renovation businesses that send quotes and often hear nothing back.

What do I send you?

A copy of the quote or a short note with the customer's first name, the job and the date you quoted. That is all we need.

Do you send the messages for me?

No. We write them and you send them from your own email or phone, so customers hear from you, not a stranger.

How fast do follow ups come back?

On Chaser Care and Chaser Plus we draft each follow up within one working day of you forwarding the quote.

Will it sound pushy?

No. Each message is short and polite and makes it easy for the customer to say yes, no or not yet.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and there is no lock in.

Can I try it first?

Yes. We will write a free follow up email for one of your recent quotes before you pay anything.
