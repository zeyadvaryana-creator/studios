FAQ

How soon can I get started?

Right away. We'll have your first reminder ready within a week of you signing up.

Can I change the reminders later?

Yes. We update your reminders whenever you change prices or services.

No. We work with most booking tools or we can set up a simple schedule for you.

How many reminders can I send?

As many as you need. There's no limit on the number of reminders or customers.

What if a customer says they don't want reminders?

We include an easy unsubscribe link in every message so customers can opt out.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

Yes. We help businesses in Australia, New Zealand, Ireland and worldwide.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel with 30 days notice. No long contracts or hidden fees.
