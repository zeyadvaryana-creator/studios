Who approves the emails?

Your clinic does. Nothing goes out until you have approved it, and we avoid any claims about outcomes.

No. We write the emails and you load them into your own booking or practice system, so patient data stays with you.

Welcome, first visit preparation, aftercare and rebook emails written for your clinic and ready to load.

What do monthly plans add?

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and there is no lock in.

Can I try it first?

Yes. We will write a free first visit welcome email for your clinic before you pay anything.
