FAQ

Who is this for?

Mechanics, tyre shops and auto electricians who want past customers to book their next service with them.

Do you send the reminders?

No. We write them and you load them into the booking or workshop system you already use, so your customer data stays with you.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. You review every reminder and we revise it until you are happy.

Do you cover local checks?

Yes. We can write reminders for logbook services, WOF, NCT or MOT style checks and tyre checks, depending on where you are.

What does the monthly plan add?

A fresh seasonal email to past customers every month, such as winter checks or holiday road trip checks.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and there is no lock in.

Can I try it first?

Yes. We will write a free service due reminder for your workshop before you pay anything.
