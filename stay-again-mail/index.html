FAQ

Who is this for?

Owners of motels, bed and breakfasts, guest houses and holiday parks who want more past guests to book direct.

Do you need my guest list?

No. We write the emails and you send them from the booking or email system you already use, so your guest data stays with you.

How often should past guests hear from us?

Usually about once a month in season and less often in quiet months. We plan it with you.

What is in the Guest Kit?

A pre arrival email, an after stay thank you and a three email come back sequence, written for your property.

Will the emails mention booking sites?

No. Each email points guests to your own website or phone number so they can book direct.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and there is no lock in.

Can I try it first?

Yes. We will write a free after stay thank you email for your property before you pay anything.
