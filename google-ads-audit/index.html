Sample work

Mini audit sample for a local gym

A small gym in Cape Town was running two search campaigns targeting generic fitness keywords. The audit showed both campaigns used the same daily budget and the same three ads. Clicks from brand terms were coming from the generic campaign, wasting thirty percent of the monthly spend. The fix list moved brand keywords into a separate campaign, paused the duplicate ads, and added location targeting around the gym postcode. After applying these steps the cost per lead dropped from seven rand to four rand within two weeks.
